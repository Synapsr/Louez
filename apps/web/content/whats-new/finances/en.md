How much did you take in today, and by which payment method? Who still owes you money? Which deposits are you holding? The new **Finances** screen answers these questions, no spreadsheet needed.

## Close your till at a glance

Pick a period: today, yesterday, the last 7 days, this month, last month, or your own dates. Four figures update: what you took in, what you refunded or gave back, the net balance, and what is still outstanding.

The **By payment method** card separates cash, card, bank transfers, cheques and online payments. That is the total to check against your cash drawer and your card terminal. Click a method to show only its movements.

## A ledger that reads like a till

Each movement shows its time, the customer, the reservation, what it is and its amount. Movements are grouped by day, with the day's balance. Money in and money out carry their sign: a refund reads “− 30.00”, linked to the original payment.

An online payment that was partly refunded shows at its original amount on the day it was paid, and the refund on the day it happened. Your totals match what really took place, day after day.

Click a row for the details: notes, original payment, refunds, Stripe identifiers to copy, invoice or credit note.

## Outstanding: who owes you money

The **Outstanding** tab lists confirmed, ongoing or completed reservations whose balance is not settled, oldest first: total, already paid, balance due and how long it has been due. One click opens the reservation to record the payment.

## Deposits: what you are holding

The **Deposits** tab brings together:

- **active holds**, with their expiry date — a warning shows two days before, since a card hold is released on its own;
- **deposits to give back**, highlighted as soon as the rental is over;
- the **history** of the latest deposit movements.

## For your accountant, and for closing the till

The **Export** menu offers a CSV file of the period, reserved for the store owner, and a **summary to print**: totals by payment method, then the list of movements. The whole team can open the screen and print the summary.
