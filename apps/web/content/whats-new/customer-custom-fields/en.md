A licence number for your scooters, a shoe size for your skis, a CPF if you rent in Brazil: every trade keeps something different about its customers. You now decide what their record holds.

## Ready-made templates

In **Settings → Customer record**, Louez suggests sets of fields based on your store's country and activity: identity document, driving licence, ski measurements, bike setup, boat licence, emergency contact, national identifiers with number verification (CPF / CNPJ, DNI / NIE, codice fiscale, NIF). One click adds them; you can then rename, reorder or archive them.

## Or your own fields

Text, number with a unit, date, choice list, yes / no, phone, email, link. For each one you choose whether it is required, whether it applies to individuals or businesses, and whether it shows on the contract and in the customer list.

A field marked **sensitive** is hidden by default on the record and left out of exports. A field included in **search** can be found by typing its value, in the list and in quick search alike, and Louez warns you when another customer already carries the same number.

## A redesigned customer record

The record gathers contact details, profile and your sections, each editable without leaving the page. The date of birth shows the age, the customer's source is picked from a list, and social profiles are added by simply pasting the profile link.

Creating a customer at the counter stays quick: additional information is folded away, to fill in now or later.
