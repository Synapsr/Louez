Until now, you signed in to Louez with a code sent by email or with Google. You can now add a password to your account. It is optional: the email code always stays available.

## Set a password

Open **My account**. The **Sign-in** card lists your sign-in methods: the email code, Google and the password. Click **Set**, choose a password of at least 8 characters, and you are done. There is a single field to fill in: the eye button lets you check what you typed.

From the same card, you can change it or remove it at any time.

## Sign in with it

On the sign-in screen, click **Sign in with a password instead**. Louez remembers the method you used: next time, the right screen opens straight away.

## Forgot your password

Click **Forgot password?**, enter the 6-digit code sent to your email, then choose a new password. You are signed in right after.

## Your account stays protected

- Every time a password is added, changed or removed, you receive an email. If you had nothing to do with it, the **This wasn't me** button removes the password and signs every device out.
- Setting or changing a password signs your other devices out.
