Coming from a spreadsheet or another tool? Bring your customers into Louez without retyping them, and without reformatting your file.

## Louez adapts to your file

In **Settings → Import**, drop a CSV file or paste the cells copied straight from Excel or Google Sheets. Louez recognises your columns (name, email, phone, address, date of birth…) whatever they are called. An unknown column can become a custom field in one click: nothing is lost.

Phone numbers are put in international format, dates and countries are read in your file's own format, and a full name is split into first and last name.

## Nothing is written before you have checked

Before importing, you see exactly what will happen: customers to create, customers to update, skipped rows and errors, each with its reason. A row in error can be fixed in place.

When a customer already exists, Louez **only fills in empty fields**: what you already entered is never changed, unless you choose to replace it.

## And every import can be undone

Each import stays in the history. You can find the customers it brought in, download the refused rows to complete them, or undo it: created records are deleted and updated records get their previous values back.
