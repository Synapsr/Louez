## Ask customers the details your rentals need

Add your own questions to the customer record, such as a licence number, a shoe size or an emergency contact, or start from a ready-made template. Choose which ones customers answer at checkout, whether they are required, and for which products: a question about the rider can be asked once per rented ski. Sensitive answers stay hidden by default on the customer record and out of exports.
