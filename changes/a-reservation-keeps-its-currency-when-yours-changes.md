## A reservation keeps its currency when your store's changes

After you change your store's currency, the reservations made before keep theirs. Deposit holds and captures, card refunds, payment requests, the online payment of a reservation, extensions, the contract, and the emails and text messages that state an amount now use the currency the reservation was booked in, not your store's current one. The reservation page of your dashboard and its edit form show it too. A card payment taken in another currency no longer counts toward what a payment request, an online payment or an extension still asks for.
