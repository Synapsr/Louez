## Try Louez in a demo store of your trade

A rental business can now try Louez in one click, without an account: /demo/ski, /demo/velo and /demo/evenementiel open a store of its own for that trade, with a catalog, invented customers and bookings, and a day already under way. The visitor books on the storefront as a customer would, with a simulated payment, then sees the booking arrive in the dashboard, checks equipment out and handles the deposit. Nothing is sent, charged or published from a demo store, and it is deleted after 24 hours. "Create my free store" leads to the sign-up.
