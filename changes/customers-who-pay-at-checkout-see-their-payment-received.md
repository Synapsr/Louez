## Customers who pay at checkout see that their payment was received

When a customer pays a booking on your online store without being signed in as that booking's customer, they come back from the payment page to a page that says their payment was received, with the booking's number and your store's name. Before, they landed on a sign-in page that said nothing about the payment. When Louez has recorded that the confirmation e-mail went out, the page says so. The page shows nothing else about the customer or the booking, and its address carries nothing that would open it again elsewhere. Its one button opens the booking after the customer signs in with a code sent to the booking's e-mail address, even when someone else is signed in on that computer.

A customer signed in as the booking's own customer still comes back to the booking itself. If Louez has not recorded the payment yet when the customer comes back, the page says the payment could not be verified yet, never that it was received.

Once the payment is recorded, the cart of the browser that made the booking is emptied, once, so the items just paid for no longer show in it. Opening someone else's link, or an old confirmation again, leaves a cart as it is. If the customer leaves the payment page without paying, the cart stays as it was and they can pick the payment up again.
