## Customers who pay at checkout see that their payment was received

When a customer pays a booking on your online store and is not signed in to their account, they come back from the payment page to a page that says their payment was received, with the booking's number and your store's name. When your store sends the confirmation e-mail, the page says it was sent. Before, they landed on a sign-in page that said nothing about the payment. The page shows nothing else about the customer or the booking. Its one button opens the booking after the customer signs in with a code sent to their e-mail address.

A customer already signed in still comes back to the booking itself. If Louez is still recording the payment when the customer comes back, the page says the payment is being confirmed, not that it was received.

Their cart is now emptied once the payment goes through, so the items they just paid for no longer show in it. If the customer leaves the payment page without paying, the cart stays as it was and they can pick the payment up again.
