## AI assistants read the same figures as your dashboard

When you ask ChatGPT, Claude or the dashboard assistant about your revenue, your bookings or your best products, they now get the figures your dashboard shows, up to the minute. Until now they read a summary built overnight, a day late and only for stores already online, so they could answer €0 while your dashboard showed payments taken that day. Revenue is the rental payments you received, card and cash alike, on the day you cashed them in, without deposits or refunds. Today and the month follow your store's time zone, on the dashboard home too. Sales show in your store's currency: if you changed currency, payments taken in the old one are left out of the totals, and the assistants tell you how much they were.
