## Editing a reservation checks your stock

When you add a product to a reservation, raise a quantity or move its dates, Louez now checks that the stock is free, as it does when you create a reservation. If something is short, you see which products and how many are left, and you can save anyway: the reservation then shows as overbooked in its history and in your inbox, until a later edit brings it back within your stock. Moving the dates of a reservation onto a busy period is no longer refused outright.

If someone else changed the reservation while you were editing it, your edit is not saved and Louez asks you to reload the page, so a total can no longer end up out of step with its lines.
