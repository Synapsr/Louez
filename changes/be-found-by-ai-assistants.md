## Be found by AI assistants

ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini now get a clear picture of your store. Your online store publishes an llms.txt page, in your store's language: who you are, where you are, your opening hours, how booking, deposits, pickup and delivery work, and every product with its rate for each rental period, linked to its page. Product pages describe each of their rates to search engines and assistants, and say when a deposit applies. On Get known, a new section shows what an assistant still lacks to recommend you (a description, your address, your opening hours, products with a photo and a price), with a button to fix each one, and counts over the last 28 days the visits that came from an AI assistant and how often their robots read your store.

Product page visits are now counted in your traffic: before, a product page reached by its readable address was not recorded.
