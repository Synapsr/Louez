## Get your first customers with "Get known"

A new page, Get known, walks you through three moves that bring the first bookings: send your store or one of its products to the people who know you (WhatsApp, SMS, email, Facebook, or a link to copy), save a QR code poster for your counter or window, and add your booking link to your Google Business Profile. Mark each step done, or "not for me" when it does not fit your shop. The page counts the visits your shared links and QR code brought over the last 28 days, by channel. Your home page shows the next step until you finish or hide it, and the Share button of your store card now uses the same links.
