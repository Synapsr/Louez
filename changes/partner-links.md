## Partner links

Campsites, hotels and tourist offices that already send you customers can now earn a commission for it. In Get known → Partners, add a partner with a percentage of the rental or a fixed amount per booking, and give them their link, a QR poster to print for their front desk, or a ready message by email or WhatsApp. A customer who opens the link is asked whether to credit that partner (the price never changes); if they agree and book within 30 days, the booking is credited automatically. You can also credit, change or remove a partner on any reservation's page. Each partner gets a monthly statement: what is due once the rental is over, what is coming, what you marked as paid and what is left, with a CSV export. Adjust a commission after a partial refund, with a reason. Send your partner their own statement link: they see their bookings' end dates, commissions and status, never anything about your customers. You pay your partners yourself; no money goes through Louez.
