## Partner links

Campsites, hotels and tourist offices that already send you customers can now earn a commission for it. In Get known → Partners, add a partner with a percentage of the rental or a fixed amount per booking, and give them their link, a QR poster to print for their front desk, or a ready message by email or WhatsApp. A customer who comes through the link and books within 30 days is credited to that partner automatically; you choose how long a link holds, from 7 to 90 days. Customers recommended by word of mouth can type the partner's code at checkout, and you can offer them one of your promo codes, applied automatically. You can also credit a partner when you create a booking yourself, or change it on any reservation's page. Each partner gets a monthly statement: what is due once the rental is over, what is coming, what you marked as paid and what is left, with a CSV export, and you can adjust a commission after a partial refund, with a reason. Your partners get their own space, without an account: what they will receive, what is coming and what they were paid, their recent bookings, their link and poster, and email notifications for each new booking, each payment and a monthly statement. They never see anything about your customers. When a month ends with commissions left to pay, it shows in Needs a look. You pay your partners yourself; no money goes through Louez. AI assistants connected to your store can read your partners and their statements.
