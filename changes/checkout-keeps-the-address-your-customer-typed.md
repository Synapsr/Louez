## Checkout keeps the address your customer typed

At checkout and in their account, the address, postal code and city your customer types for themselves are saved as typed, apart from surrounding spaces. Leaving the field, pressing Continue or a slow or failed address search no longer swaps them for another address. A suggestion fills them only when the customer picks it from the list. If they then correct the postal code or the city, their correction stays, and the delivery step no longer uses the location of the address they picked.

In every address field, delivery and store addresses included, an address answer that arrives after the person typed something else is now ignored.
