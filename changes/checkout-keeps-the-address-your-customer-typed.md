## Checkout keeps the address your customer typed

At checkout, the address, postal code and city your customer types for themselves stay as typed. Leaving the field or pressing Enter no longer swaps them for another address, and neither does a search that answers late or fails. A suggestion fills them only when the customer picks it from the list, and a pick that is still loading when anything in the three fields changes fills nothing. After a pick, a postal code or a city the customer corrected stays when they edit the street, and the picked location is dropped as soon as the address differs from it.

In every address field, an address answer that arrives after the person typed something else, or after they left the page step, is now ignored, and an old list of suggestions no longer replaces a newer one.
