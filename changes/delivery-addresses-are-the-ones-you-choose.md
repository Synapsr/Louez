## Delivery addresses are the ones you or your customer choose

The address fields that need a precise location (the delivery and collection address at checkout and on a reservation you create or edit, your pickup points and the delivery simulator, your store's address at onboarding and on your online store's contact page) no longer swap what was typed for the first address the search finds. Before, leaving such a field could quietly replace the text with another address, and the delivery fee was then computed from a place nobody chose.

Now the typed text stays, and the field says under it to choose the address from the list, or to place it on the map where the field offers one. Coming back to the field, or pressing Enter, shows the suggestions for that text again; the arrow keys and Enter pick one. Until an address is chosen, no delivery fee is computed: the checkout's delivery step cannot continue and a reservation you create cannot be saved, as before when the address was missing. When you edit a reservation, each delivery or collection address must be chosen from the list or placed on the map before the reservation can be saved, or that leg moved back to the store.

At checkout, choosing delivery now starts the address from the one your customer gave, even when they typed it rather than picking it: a tap on the field shows the matching suggestions to confirm it. An address search that does not answer within 10 seconds is given up, so the field never stays stuck on its spinner, and pressing Enter or coming back to the field searches again. Addresses already saved with their location are left as they are.
