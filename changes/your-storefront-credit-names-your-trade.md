## Your storefront's "Powered by" line names your trade

On Louez Cloud, the "Powered by" line at the bottom of your storefront now says what Louez is for your kind of shop, in your visitor's language. A bike shop reads "Powered by Louez, bike rental software", and the link opens the Louez page for bike rental. Ski, tool, watersport and sound-and-lighting shops get the same line for their trade. Louez reads the trade from your catalog's products and categories, and only when one trade clearly leads. Other shops read "Powered by Louez", which links to the Louez home page. Self-hosted instances keep their "Powered by Louez.io" line.
