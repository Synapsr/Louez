## Confirm a reservation over your stock

Accepting a request or confirming a quote that your stock no longer covers is no longer a dead end. Louez shows you which products are short, with the size or colour each line asked for, and how many are left, and you can confirm it anyway: the reservation then shows as overbooked in its history and in your inbox, as when you create or edit one over your stock. This works from the reservations list, a product's reservations and the reservation's page, and a paid request that lost its stock can now be confirmed this way.

When it is the turnover buffer you keep between two rentals that stops lines of the reservation, Louez lists them and lets you confirm anyway, with less time between those rentals: the history marks it as a short turnaround, and the confirmation adds nothing to your inbox since you chose it. For now, editing that reservation, or the one right next to it, can still list it in your inbox as overbooked. A reservation short both ways asks you twice, once for the buffer and once for the stock. A unit you assigned to the reservation that another booking needs, and a consumable you have run out of, still stop the confirmation: remove or replace the unit, then confirm again.

An assistant or an app connected to your store can confirm over your stock or past the buffer too. Louez first gives it the list of products that are short and asks it to confirm; Louez cannot check that it showed you that list. Your in-app assistant leaves that choice to you on the reservation's page. Your customers cannot accept a quote your stock or your buffer does not cover, and a payment does not confirm such a request: they wait until you confirm it yourself or free up the stock.
