## Confirm a reservation over your stock

Accepting a request or confirming a quote that your stock no longer covers is no longer a dead end. Louez shows you which products are short and how many are left, and you can confirm it anyway: the reservation then shows as overbooked in its history and in your inbox, as when you create or edit one over your stock. This works from the reservations list, a product's reservations and the reservation's page, and a paid request that lost its stock can now be confirmed this way.

When the stock covers the reservation but not the turnover buffer you keep between two rentals, Louez says so and does not offer to confirm anyway: free up stock around those dates or move them. A unit already out with another booking and a consumable you have run out of still stop the confirmation.

An assistant or an app connected to your store can confirm over your stock too, only after showing you each product that is short; your in-app assistant leaves that choice to you on the reservation's page. Your customers cannot accept a quote your stock does not cover: they are told it is no longer available until you confirm it or free up the stock.
