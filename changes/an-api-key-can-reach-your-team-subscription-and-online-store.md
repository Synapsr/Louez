## An API key can now reach your team, subscription and online store

When you create an API key in Settings > Integrations > MCP, you can now give it rights on your online store, your team, your subscription and your integrations, as read only or read and write, like the other areas. "Full access" now includes them for the keys you create from now on, and "Read only" reads them too. Before, a key made in the dashboard could not reach these areas even with full access, and its calls to them were refused with `PERMISSION_DENIED`.

Keys you already have keep the rights they had: create a new key to give these areas to an integration. Removing a member, cancelling an invitation and adding the card Louez bills you on still ask for your approval or hand you a page, whatever the key holds.
