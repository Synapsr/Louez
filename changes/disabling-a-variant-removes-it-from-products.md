## Variants are managed per product, with a one-click withdrawal

Switching off a variant such as Size used to hide it in the dashboard while the online store kept splitting stock by it and kept asking for it on the home page. Switching a variant off now only stops offering it for new products: the products that use it keep it everywhere. To take a variant out of your products, remove it in each product form, or use the new "Remove from products" action in the variant manager, which lists the products and explains what changes before you confirm. Stock is counted as one pool again and upcoming bookings can be served by any unit. A variant still used by products can no longer be deleted by mistake. A product whose units carry a size or colour it never declared now shows that variant in its form, as the online store already did, so saving no longer erases the values. Adding a variant to a product that already has bookings taken without one now saves: those bookings can be served by any unit, and the online store counts them against the whole product so no unit is offered twice.
