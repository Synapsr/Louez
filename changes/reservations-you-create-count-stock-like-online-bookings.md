## Reservations you create count your stock like online bookings

When you create a reservation in the dashboard, Louez now counts your stock exactly as it does for an online booking. A marketplace booking whose hold has expired no longer blocks your dates, and a line with no size chosen is accepted whenever the units you have left cover it, whatever their sizes, instead of asking you to confirm an overbooking; a quote created that way can be confirmed too. When a consumable is short of what you have on hand, Louez now tells you so.
