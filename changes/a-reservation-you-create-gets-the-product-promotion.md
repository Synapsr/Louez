## A reservation you create by hand gets the product's promotion

A reservation you create from the dashboard now gets a product's promotion, as a booking on your online store does: the same product, dates and quantity cost the same in both. A promotion counts when it runs on the day you create the reservation. The new reservation form shows the price before the promotion struck through, next to the price your customer pays. A price you type by hand is never discounted. The reservation keeps its promotion when you change its dates or quantities later, even after the promotion has ended.
