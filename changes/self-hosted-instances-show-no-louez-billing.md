## Self-host Louez with every feature unlocked and no Louez billing in sight

With billing off (the default), every store gets every feature with no plan limit, and the plan, billing, referral and platform-admin pages, the upgrade and referral prompts, Louez Cloud's terms of service and its offer on the sign-in page are gone; the sign-in page still links the privacy policy. Online payments now only need `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, without plan prices, and the dashboard AI assistant works for every store once an AI provider key is set. A multi-store self-host no longer names LumyCloud as the host in its legal notices; Louez Cloud sets `LOUEZ_HOSTED_BY=lumycloud`.
