## A deposit hold is placed once on the customer's card

Asking for a deposit hold twice at the same moment, with a second click, from two tabs or through an assistant, now places a single hold on the customer's card. When Stripe is slow to answer or fails, Louez asks about the same hold instead of placing another: until Stripe confirms or refuses it, the reservation says the hold is not confirmed, your inbox shows it, and no second hold is placed. Releasing or capturing a hold only ever touches that hold: one your customer authorized in the meantime stays on their card and on the reservation. A second authorization of a deposit already held is released, and Louez keeps asking Stripe until it is; the payment history shows it as being released, never as a hold, and your inbox tells you when Stripe has not confirmed the release after half an hour. When Stripe never confirms a hold you asked for, the reservation's deposit section lets you settle it: paste the ID of the payment whose details in Stripe show this reservation, and Louez records it once it matches; or, after checking the Stripe dashboard of the account the hold was asked on, tell Louez you found nothing and ask for a new hold. An authorization in Stripe that does not show this reservation's reference is not this hold: release or capture it from Stripe. Telling Louez you found nothing cancels nothing at Stripe: should that hold appear later, Louez records it when Stripe reports it, as the reservation's deposit if it has none, and releases it if another hold already secures it, so a wrong answer can leave an extra hold on your customer's card for a while. A failed attempt no longer warns you once another hold succeeded, and the payment history keeps only the latest failed attempt.

What is not covered yet: a hold your customer gives on their own page while one you asked for is still unconfirmed can place two holds; the second is released. The expiry date shown for a hold is the date the bank gives it up, and a hold placed before you changed Stripe account can still be captured or released. Deleting your account also drops any release Louez still owed: such an extra hold then stays on your customer's card until it expires.
