## A deposit hold is placed once on the customer's card

Asking for a deposit hold twice at the same moment, with a second click, from two tabs or through an assistant, now places a single hold on the customer's card. When Stripe is slow to answer or fails, Louez asks about the same hold instead of placing another: until Stripe confirms or refuses it, the reservation says the hold is not confirmed, your inbox shows it, and no second hold is placed. Releasing or capturing a hold only ever touches that hold: one your customer authorized in the meantime stays on their card and on the reservation. A second authorization of a deposit already held is released, and Louez keeps asking Stripe until it is; the payment history shows it as being released, never as a hold, and your inbox tells you when Stripe has not confirmed the release after half an hour. When Stripe never confirms a hold you asked for, the reservation's deposit section lets you close the request, after checking the Payments of the Stripe dashboard of the account it was asked on. Louez first looks for that hold itself and records it if it finds it and it matches your request; otherwise the request is closed and you can ask for a new hold. If Louez finds a hold for your request that no longer matches it (its amount, card or reference was changed in Stripe), the request stays open and Louez tells you: cancel that hold in Stripe, then close the request again; until Stripe shows it cancelled, Louez does not close the request, even when a later search no longer finds that hold. If you capture it in Stripe instead, Louez does not record that amount and the request stays open: contact Louez support. Closing cancels nothing at Stripe: should that hold appear later and match your request, Louez records it when Stripe reports it, as the reservation's deposit if it has none, and releases it if another hold already secures it, so closing while a hold exists can leave an extra hold on your customer's card for a while. An authorization you see in Stripe that Louez has not recorded is handled from Stripe: cancel it there. A failed attempt no longer warns you once another hold succeeded, and the payment history keeps only the latest failed attempt.

What is not covered yet: a hold your customer gives on their own page while one you asked for is still unconfirmed can place two holds; the second is released. The expiry date shown for a hold is the date the bank gives it up, and a hold placed before you changed Stripe account can still be captured or released. Deleting your account also drops any release Louez still owed: such an extra hold then stays on your customer's card until it expires.
