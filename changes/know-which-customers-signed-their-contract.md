## Know which customers signed their contract, and get the others to sign

Each reservation now shows whether the customer signed the rental contract, and when and how. A contract you confirm is no longer counted as signed by the customer. Customers sign when they book online, on the payment page or from the link in their emails, or in store by drawing their signature on your device. In your reservation settings, choose whether a pickup without a signature is allowed, triggers a warning, or is blocked until the customer signs.
