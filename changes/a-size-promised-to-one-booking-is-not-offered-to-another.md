## A size promised to one booking is no longer offered to another

When a reservation asks for a size without choosing its colour, or leaves any other choice open, Louez now keeps units of that size for it: a later booking that needs them is refused online, and asks you to confirm an overbooking in the dashboard, instead of promising more units than you have. Confirming a quote or a request whose assigned unit has since gone to another booking is refused too, and tells you which unit to replace. Assigning a unit that such a booking needs tells you your stock is short.
