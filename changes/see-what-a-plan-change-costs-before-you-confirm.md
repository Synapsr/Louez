## See what a plan change costs before you confirm it

When you pick another plan or billing interval, the dialog now shows the amount charged today, or the day the new plan starts. A change that costs more is charged at once and applies right away. A cheaper one starts at the end of the period you have paid for, so you keep your current plan until then: the plan page shows the pending change, and you can keep your current plan instead.
