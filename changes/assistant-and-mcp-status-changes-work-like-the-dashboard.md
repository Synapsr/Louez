## Status changes from the assistant or MCP work like the dashboard

When you confirm, cancel or reject a reservation through the dashboard assistant or an MCP client, Louez now records or removes its fees as the dashboard does. Until now these changes were not billed. On pay-as-you-go, a confirmation adds the reservation fee, or uses one of your free reservations while you have some; on a marketplace booking, it also adds the marketplace fee, whatever your plan. Each change also appears in the reservation's history under your name, with the rejection reason you gave through MCP, and the marketplace now picks up status changes made this way on its bookings.
