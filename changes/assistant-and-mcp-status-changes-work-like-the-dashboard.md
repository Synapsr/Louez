## Status changes from the assistant or MCP work like the dashboard

When you confirm, reject, cancel, pick up or return a reservation through the dashboard assistant or an MCP client, Louez now does what the dashboard does: it refuses a confirmation when an item is already booked for those dates, sends your customer the same emails, sends you the same notifications, and creates or cancels the Tulip insurance contract. It also records or removes the fees as the dashboard does; until now these changes were not billed. On pay-as-you-go, a confirmation adds the reservation fee, or uses one of your free reservations while you have some, and a marketplace booking also adds the marketplace fee, whatever your plan. Each change appears in the reservation's history under your name, with the rejection reason you gave through MCP, which your customer also receives in the rejection email as on the dashboard, and the marketplace now picks up status changes made this way on its bookings.
